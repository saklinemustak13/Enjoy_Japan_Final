JAPANESE CULTURE

Small customs, big respect

Japanese culture is built on care for others. Knowing a few customs helps you feel at home.

  • 礼Bow or nod
    A light bow is a normal greeting.
  • 列Wait in line
    At stations, shops, and restaurants.
  • 時Be on time
    Punctuality shows respect.

FEATURED TIPS

Tips for foreigners in Japan

Short reminders for living in Japan.

  • 01

    Carry a little cash. Many small shops and restaurants still do not accept cards.

  • 02

    Get an IC card such as Suica or PASMO for trains, buses, and convenience stores.

  • 03

    Save the emergency numbers: 110 for police and 119 for fire or ambulance.

  • 04

    Check your city's garbage calendar. Collection days depend on the area.

  • 05

    Last trains leave around midnight. Check the time before you go out at night.
